About Us

The Vision Behind PHI

We are more than a production house — we are storytellers, visual artists, and creative strategists.

Our Story

Founded by Kishu Gupta with a passion for visual storytelling, PHI Production House has grown from a small editing studio to a full-service content creation agency. We believe every frame matters, every cut tells a story, and every project deserves cinematic excellence.

Our Mission

To empower brands and creators with visual content that resonates, engages, and inspires action.

Our Approach

We blend creativity with strategy, ensuring every project not only looks stunning but also achieves measurable results.
